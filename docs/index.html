How to play

1 Clone

Pick one of your pieces and tap a touching hex. A copy appears there — your piece stays put.

2 Jump

Or tap a hex two steps away. Your piece leaps there, leaving an empty hex behind.

3 Flip & claim

Every enemy piece touching where you land flips to your colour. When the board fills up, most hexes wins. Can't move? Your rival claims every empty hex!
